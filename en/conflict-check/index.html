Conflict Check

Bug or Feature?

Conflict isn't the problem. Conflict that's stuck is. In six minutes the check shows where your conflict has locked, whether it needs stirring up or calming down, and which skill that takes. Nothing is stored or sent.

Basis: Klaus Eidenschink, "Die Kunst des Konflikts" (The Art of Conflict, Carl-Auer, German only). The nine guiding distinctions are his; the questions, scoring and wording are Jacob Noon's. The check is a teaching tool, not a diagnosis.
